📜 Calculator Logic

  • FN = Chaldean total of First Name.
  • NN = Chaldean total of Full Name.
  • PN = Final Pyramid Number.
  • IN = Total Chaldean numerical value of all vowels in Full Name.
  • EN = Total Chaldean numerical value of all consonants in Full Name.
  • HN = Total number of alphabets in Full Name.
  • IN, EN and HN are NOT reduced.
  • BN is NOT reduced. If the birth day is 22, BN remains 22.
  • DN is NOT reduced. The complete DOB digit total is retained.
  • All numbers are displayed in two-digit format. For example, 7 is displayed as 07.
  • Intermediate Pyramid calculations are reduced to a single digit.
  • The final Pyramid Number is NOT reduced.
  • Golden Rajyog: BN = FN = NN = PN = IN = EN = HN OR DN = FN = NN = PN = IN = EN = HN.
  • Silver Rajyog: FN = NN = PN = IN = EN = HN.
  • If both conditions are satisfied, both Golden Rajyog and Silver Rajyog will be displayed.
